                                              User Requirement Document (URD)
                      Automated CTC (Cost to Company) Generator & Compensation Structuring System

1. Executive Summary
This document defines the functional, structural and computational requirements for an enterprise CTC Generator that will replace
the current manual spreadsheets and macro-based calculators used for offer preparation.
The system will organise every compensation element into five groups: Inclusion Wages, Exclusion Wages, Statutory Payments,
Policy-Based Payments and Variable Pay. It will enforce the following business rules automatically:
    •   Exact parity between the Inclusion and Exclusion groups.
    •   Qualification-based gating of the Professional Allowance.
    •   Grade- and location-driven allowances.
    •   Provident Fund (PF) logic with ceiling tiers.
    •   optional, tax-regime-dependent NPS calculation.
The objective is an accurate, audit-ready and standardised process for generating CTC breakups and offer letters, with manual
calculation risk removed.

2. Objectives and Scope

2.1 Objectives
    •   Replace error-prone spreadsheet and macro calculators with a single controlled tool.
    •   Standardise offer generation across grades and locations.
    •   Guarantee that every CTC breakup balances exactly and follows statutory rules.
    •   Provide a clear audit trail of inputs, rules applied and outputs.

2.2 In Scope
    •   Candidate profile and tax-regime inputs.
    •   Grade-, location- and qualification-driven component configuration.
    •   Sequential (non-circular) calculation engine.
    •   Live parity check between Inclusion and Exclusion groups.
    •   Output of the CTC Breakup Sheet and Offer Letter (PDF).

2.3 Out of Scope (for this release)
    •   Payroll processing and payslip generation.
    •   Integration with HRMS/ATS (to be considered in a later phase).
    •   Month-wise tax computation and investment declarations.

4. Core Calculation Rules

4.1 Mandatory Parity Constraint
The total of the Inclusion Wages Group must exactly equal the total of the Exclusion Wages Group:
Inclusion Wages Group = Exclusion Wages Group
Together, the two groups make up monthly Gross Wages. Gross is the primary input and is split equally, so each group equals
Gross ÷ 2 (for example, a Gross of ₹1,00,000 gives ₹50,000 in each group). The system must not allow a CTC to be generated if
the two totals differ.

4.2 Gross-Driven Sequential Calculation
Monthly Gross is entered first, which fixes both group totals before any component is calculated. Special Allowance then balances
the Inclusion group and HRA balances the Exclusion group. Because the Inclusion total is known up front (Gross ÷ 2), Employer PF
can be calculated directly with no circular dependency. CTC is treated as an output (Gross plus Statutory, Policy-Based and
Variable Pay); a CTC-target mode is deferred to a later phase.
Required implementation: the engine must run in a strict, step-by-step sequence and must not rely on live circular formulas.
    1.      Input. Gross is entered; Inclusion target = Exclusion target = Gross ÷ 2.
    2.      Inclusion fixed values. Set Basic (within the grade range) and Professional Allowance (if qualified).
    3.      Exclusion fixed values. Compute Conveyance, Employer PF, NPS (zero if not opted in) and Superannuation.
    4.      Special Allowance. Balance the Inclusion group.
    5.      HRA. Balance the Exclusion group.
Special Allowance = (Gross ÷ 2) − Basic − Professional Allowance
HRA = (Gross ÷ 2) − Conveyance − Employer PF − NPS − Superannuation
HRA is not capped at a percentage of Basic. The system should raise an error if either balancing plug turns out negative.

5. User Workflow and UI Specifications

5.1 Candidate Profile and Tax Input
The user selects:
    •   Monthly Gross Wages
    •   Candidate Grade
    •   Job Location
    •   Professional Qualification
    •   Tax Regime (Old or New) and NPS opt-in (Yes or No)

5.2 Dynamic Slabs and Sequential Engine Execution
    •   Gross is split equally into the Inclusion and Exclusion targets.
    •   Basic salary ranges and allowances populate automatically per grade.
    •   Professional Allowance locks or opens based on the qualification selected.
    •   Conveyance, PF and NPS compute through the sequential pipeline.
    •   Special Allowance and HRA balance their groups automatically.

5.3 Parity Check Indicator
A live visual indicator displays Inclusion Group Total vs. Exclusion Group Total and confirms absolute balance. Generation of
outputs is blocked if the totals do not match.

5.4 Output Generation
The system generates:
    •   A structured CTC Breakup Sheet showing all five component sections.
    •   An Offer Letter (PDF) reflecting the same figures.

7. Non-Functional Requirements
     •   Accuracy: results must match approved manual calculations to the rupee.
     •   Configurability: grade matrices, slabs, percentages and statutory ceilings must be maintainable by authorised HR users
         without code changes.
     •   Auditability: every generated CTC must be traceable to its inputs and the rule versions used.
     •   Access control: role-based access, with edit rights limited to Compensation & Benefits.
     •   Usability: a single-screen workflow that a non-technical HR user can complete without training beyond a short
         walkthrough.

8. Assumptions and Open Items for Confirmation
     •   The ESI wage ceiling and statutory rates (ESI 3.25%, Gratuity 4.81%) must be kept configurable, since statutory limits can
         change.
     •   Superannuation percentages by grade are to be supplied by Compensation & Benefits.
     •   Annual Special Allowance is listed under Statutory Payments in this draft; its classification should be confirmed, as it is a
         policy-driven payout slab.
     •   The grade matrix (Basic ranges, Conveyance caps, Professional Allowance slabs, Variable Pay bands) will be provided as
         a master data annexure.
     •   How Basic is set within the grade range (entered by the user, or defaulted as a share of Gross) is to be confirmed.
     •   Finance / Tax should confirm that an uncapped HRA is acceptable, as the HRA tax exemption is limited under the Old Tax
         Regime.
     •   A CTC-target mode (entering a CTC and solving back to Gross) is deferred; confirm whether it is needed in a later phase.
