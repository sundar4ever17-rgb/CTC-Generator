1. Project Overview
Develop the existing CTC Generator demo into a production application for preparing accurate, consistent 
CTC breakups and offer letters. The application will replace manual spreadsheets with a controlled, single-
screen workflow for HR and Compensation & Benefits (C&B), following the supplied User Requirement 
Document (URD).

3. Proposed Development Scope
Build on the existing application: complete the URD rules using approved client data; resolve the identified 
calculation and access decisions; apply the approved document template; test calculations, permissions 
and outputs; and prepare production deployment and handover. Configuration of grades, slabs, 
percentages and ceilings will remain available to authorised users without code changes.
Outside this release: payroll, payslips, HRMS/ATS integration, month-wise tax calculations, investment 
declarations, and CTC-target reverse calculation.

Business rules follow the supplied URD; final values and conventions require client approval.
Requirement
Business rule to retain and validate
Gross and balance
Monthly Gross is the input. Inclusion = Exclusion = Gross / 2. Calculate targets, 
Inclusion fixed values, Exclusion fixed values, Special Allowance, then HRA, 
without circular formulas.
Balancing 
amounts
Special Allowance = Gross / 2 - Basic - Professional Allowance. HRA = Gross / 2 - 
Conveyance - Employer PF - NPS - Superannuation, with no Basic-based cap. 
Block output for unequal totals or negative balancing amounts.
Grade and 
qualification
Validate Basic against grade limits; apply grade-based Conveyance caps. 
Professional Allowance applies only to CA, CMA or CS qualifications using the 
approved slab.
Employer PF
If Basic > INR 25,000: 12% of Basic. Otherwise, if 50% of Inclusion <= INR 25,000: 
12% of that amount. Otherwise: INR 3,000.
NPS and statutory 
rules
NPS: 14% of Basic under New Regime or 10% under Old Regime, only when 
opted in; otherwise zero. URD rates: Employer ESI 3.25% within the applicable 
wage limit; Gratuity 4.81% of Inclusion. Keep rates and ceilings configurable.
Other payments 
and CTC
Apply approved Superannuation, annual allowance, conditional policy payments 
and grade-based variable-pay schedules. CTC adds Statutory, Policy-Based and 
Variable Pay to Gross using confirmed periods. Employer PF, NPS and 
Superannuation are already within Gross.
Documents and 
traceability
Show the same approved figures in the CTC sheet and offer PDF. Retain inputs, 
rule versions and outputs for audit, with role-based editing controls.

8. Assumptions and Client Requirements
Client to provide the approved grade matrix: Basic ranges, Conveyance caps, Professional Allowance slabs 
and Variable Pay bands/schedules.
Client to supply Superannuation rules; ESI wage ceiling and basis; Annual Special Allowance 
slabs/classification; and eligibility, amounts and periods for policy payments.
Client to confirm Basic selection, rounding to the rupee, annualisation and payment-period conventions; 
Finance/Tax to confirm acceptance of uncapped HRA.
Client to provide the official offer wording, company details, required candidate/employment fields and 
signatory details.
Client to confirm HR/C&B edit rights, provide authorised reviewers, approved calculation examples, production 
environment access and UAT sign-off. Missing policies will not be treated as zero or inferred from demo/test 
data.
9. Conclusion
The existing demo provides a practical foundation for the production CTC Generator. The proposed work 
will complete the client configuration, validate the URD calculations and documents, and prepare the 
application for operational use. Development dates and commercial terms remain subject to agreement.
Proposal basis: supplied five-page CTC Generator URD; demo README; requirements, implementation-decisions and verification 
documents.
