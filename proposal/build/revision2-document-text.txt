1. Project Overview
Develop the existing demo into a production application for Brakes India to prepare consistent CTC 
breakups and offer letters. This proposal covers requirements confirmation, UI and backend completion, 
database preparation, salary rules, testing, client acceptance, deployment and go-live. The estimate credits 
the existing implementation and covers the remaining production work; previous demo development is not 
charged again.
Basis: the supplied five-page URD, existing demo source and documentation, and the requested estimation and support 
updates. All amounts are proposed planning estimates, subject to confirmation of the stated assumptions at the end of Week 
1.

Reuse the current Next.js, Go and PostgreSQL application. Complete agreed UI and API adjustments; load

Business rules below follow the supplied URD; client-approved policy values and calculation conventions remain

Requirement
Business rule to retain and validate
Gross and balance
Monthly Gross is the input. Inclusion = Exclusion = Gross / 2. Calculate targets, 
Inclusion fixed values, Exclusion fixed values, Special Allowance, then HRA, without 
circular formulas.
Balancing amounts
Special Allowance = Gross / 2 - Basic - Professional Allowance. HRA = Gross / 2 - 
Conveyance - Employer PF - NPS - Superannuation, with no Basic-based cap. Block 
output for unequal totals or negative balancing amounts.
Grade and 
qualification
Validate Basic against grade limits; apply grade-based Conveyance caps. Professional 
Allowance applies only to CA, CMA or CS qualifications using the approved slab.
Employer PF
If Basic > INR 25,000: 12% of Basic. Otherwise, if 50% of Inclusion <= INR 25,000: 
12% of that amount. Otherwise: INR 3,000.
NPS and statutory 
rules
NPS: 14% of Basic under New Regime or 10% under Old Regime, only when opted in; 
otherwise zero. URD rates: Employer ESI 3.25% within the applicable wage limit; 
Gratuity 4.81% of Inclusion. Keep rates and ceilings configurable.
Other payments 
and CTC
Apply approved Superannuation, annual allowance, conditional policy payments and 
grade-based variable-pay schedules. CTC adds Statutory, Policy-Based and Variable 
Pay to Gross using confirmed periods. Employer PF, NPS and Superannuation are 
already within Gross.
Documents and 
traceability
Show the same approved figures in the CTC sheet and offer PDF. Retain inputs, rule 
versions and outputs for audit, with role-based editing controls.

8. Assumptions and Client Requirements
8.1 Client inputs and availability
By the end of Week 1: approve the grade matrix, Basic ranges/selection, Conveyance caps, Professional Allowance 
slabs, Variable Pay bands and LTM schedules.
Confirm Superannuation, ESI wage ceiling/basis, Annual Special Allowance slabs/classification, policy-payment 
eligibility/amounts/periods, rounding and annualisation. Finance/Tax confirms uncapped HRA.
Supply the official Brakes India CTC/offer formats, required fields, signatory details, HR/C&B edit permissions and 
approved manual calculation examples. Missing rules are not inferred from demo or test data.
Nominate HR/C&B, Finance and IT decision-makers. Return consolidated decisions within two working days and 
provide reviewers for two five-working-day UAT cycles in Weeks 8 and 9.
Provide source access and deployment constraints at kickoff; ready the UAT environment by Week 7 and production 
access before Week 9. Delayed inputs or approvals move dates and may require re-planning.
8.2 Scope and environment assumptions
One Brakes India organisation, an English web application, one CTC format and one offer template; retain the 
existing technology stack and single-screen workflow.
One UAT environment and one production environment. One clean, approved master-data set, one initial production 
load and one correction pass are included; historical candidate/payroll migration is excluded.
Policies fit the existing fixed, percentage, conditional and scheduled rule types, with bounded changes within the 
URD. Final matrices, data volume, concurrency and retention requirements are confirmed in Week 1.
Existing role-based login is retained. Corporate SSO, high availability/disaster-recovery infrastructure, external 
integrations and independent penetration-test certification are not included; mandatory client IT requirements must 
be assessed before baseline approval.
8.3 Testing, UAT and final approval
Internal tests cover all 20 components, grade/qualification boundaries, all PF branches, both NPS regimes/opt-out, 
ESI eligibility, balancing failures, annual payments, permissions, saved revisions and matching PDFs.
UAT cycle 1 validates real client scenarios; cycle 2 verifies consolidated feedback and fixes. Approved manual 
results must match to the rupee using the agreed rounding convention.
Release requires written HR/C&B acceptance, approved masters/template, no open critical or high-severity defects, 
and an agreed disposition for any minor issues.
8.4 Production setup and go-live verification
Before release: validate production access, TLS/secrets, controlled database migrations, backup/restore, 
logs/monitoring and rollback; remove demo credentials/data, load and reconcile approved masters and users. After 
deployment: verify login and permissions, representative calculations, saved revisions, CTC/offer PDFs and audit 
history; record joint business/IT go-live approval.
9. Conclusion
Plan for 10 weeks to production go-live, with a 10–12-week delivery window and a recommended budget of INR 
13,80,000 including contingency and a 90-day defect warranty, excluding taxes and infrastructure. Optional 
maintenance is INR 24,000/month after warranty. Confirm the pending policies, environment requirements and 
commercial basis in Week 1 before fixing the delivery baseline.
